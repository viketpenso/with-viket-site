A $373 Billion Market

The U.S. apparel market is estimated to generate $373 billion in revenue in 2026. A significant opportunity—but market size alone doesn't guarantee commercial success.

Entering the U.S. is more than translating a line sheet, converting prices into dollars, or attending a trade show. It's about whether your product, pricing, positioning, and commercial strategy fit the market you're entering.
